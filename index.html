BCV702 – Estimation and Contract Management

Programme: B.E. Civil Engineering

University: Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)

Semester: VII Semester

Credits: 4

Teaching Hours: 5 Hours/Week

Course Overview

This course introduces students to estimation, quantity surveying, rate analysis, specifications, tendering, contract management, and valuation of civil engineering works. Students develop the skills required to prepare estimates, analyze construction costs, and understand construction contracts.

Course Outcomes

  • Prepare quantity estimates and cost abstracts.
  • Develop estimates for buildings, roads, water supply and sanitary works.
  • Prepare specifications and perform rate analysis.
  • Understand tendering and contract management.
  • Prepare valuation reports for buildings.
